# Humanity's Last Quiz

Objective:
A weekly summary of all the things my pub quiz team and I might get asked
during our weekly quiz.
The topics always seem to cover the same few categories so it might be a nice
way to scoop up some quick points if we can bolster our topical knowledge on a
few question staples.

Here are the things you can usually expect to be questioned on:
- Births and deaths of celebrities/historical figures that have happened on
  that day/within a week or two of that day.
- Billboard top song/album/artist of the day/within a week or two of that day.
- Box Office top grossing movie of the day/within a week or two of that day.


Some fun things I want to include at random:
- Top 10s
    - The top 10 ranked {countries by landmass, movies of the 90s according to
      the NYTimes, countries that speak French, top grossing movie stars,
      oldest presidents, etc)
- A random group of trivia facts for the day
- A few geography facts

I have a few ideas on how to gather and aggregate this information but have no
inkling on how to best make a website to showcase it at a glance in a visually
appealing style.
- Wikipedia has a 'on this day', other websites may have an API call I can do
  for free to get the on this day facts going back from the day we're on to 7
      days before then. We can use this to get famous birthdays from
      antiquity-1970s.
- For famous birthdays after the 70s, wikipedia may not be tracking all the
  celebrities on netflix/youtube (maybe they are, confirm). Therefore, we may
  need to also parse a tabloid website for births/deaths within the last 7
  days.
- There has to be a good API service (free) to get the top billboard
  single/album/artist of the last 7 days. Same for box office movies.
- We can find a few trivia websites to query for fun trivia facts.
